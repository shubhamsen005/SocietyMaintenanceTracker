# 2–3 minute evaluator walkthrough

1. Open the app and select **Continue as Admin**. The command center immediately shows operational KPIs, overdue work and the critical Tower B Lift 2 incident.
2. Select the Lift 2 incident. Explain that four individual complaints stay intact, but Incident Fusion identifies one underlying failure with an 87% explainable match.
3. Open the resolution trail. Point out actor-stamped history, SLA time, risk reasons and the resolve-with-proof action.
4. Visit **Assets**. Tower B Lift 2 has a passport, health score and QR-sheet action; its QR URL routes residents to prefilled reporting.
5. Visit **Intelligence** for recurrence and first-time-fix indicators, then **Notice board** for pinned, delivery-aware communication.
6. Sign in as **Continue as Resident**. Show the simpler view, status timeline and transparent shared incident context.
7. Choose **Raise a complaint**. Walk through the three steps and note that analysis offers a duplicate suggestion without forcing a merge.
